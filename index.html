Montpelier, VT — Property Finder

ChangelogSuggest a feature / report a bug

Property type, listing status, and flood risk are now filtered from their column headers in the table (click the ▾ next to the column name).

Source: Vermont statewide parcel/Grand List data (VCGI) joined with Montpelier assessor record cards (Patriot Properties) and Realtor.com listing data. Listing status/market value cover properties matched to a Realtor.com record from the last 10 years — most properties won't have one. For those, the Est. Value shown is a rough extrapolation instead (assessed value × the typical ratio of real Realtor.com estimates to assessed value in town, shown in italics) — not a real market estimate.
